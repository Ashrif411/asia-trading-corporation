About Asia Trading Corporation

Reliable Bangladesh-based partner for international apparel buyers.

Asia Trading Corporation supports foreign RMG buyers and importers by placing, managing, and coordinating apparel orders with garment manufacturers in Bangladesh. Our role is to reduce sourcing complexity, improve communication, monitor production progress, and help buyers receive export-ready garments on time.

Mission

To provide transparent, disciplined, and buyer-focused apparel sourcing support from Bangladesh.

Vision

To become a trusted international bridge between global fashion buyers and reliable Bangladeshi garment manufacturers.

Goals

To strengthen buyer confidence through quality, compliance awareness, timely communication, and shipment discipline.

Why choose us?

  • Direct coordination with selected garment factories.
  • Strong understanding of Bangladesh RMG production workflows.
  • Support for sample development, merchandising, QC, and shipment follow-up.
  • Professional communication for buyers across time zones.
Our Services

End-to-end RMG sourcing and export management support.

Services designed for brands, importers, wholesalers, and sourcing agents who need a dependable Bangladesh-based operational partner.

Apparel Sourcing

Factory identification, product feasibility checks, quotation collection, and supplier matching for buyer requirements.

Order Management

Order tracking, T&A follow-up, documentation support, and production communication from PO to shipment.

Quality Control

Inline, midline, pre-final, and final inspection coordination to support buyer quality expectations.

Production Monitoring

Regular factory follow-up, risk reporting, progress tracking, and issue escalation before delays become critical.

Compliance Verification

Factory profile review, documentation checking, audit coordination, and compliance communication support.

Logistics Coordination

Shipment planning, packing follow-up, forwarder communication, and export document coordination support.

Supplier Communication

Professional communication between buyers, factories, suppliers, testing labs, and logistics partners.

Merchandising Support

Sample follow-up, BOM coordination, costing support, trim and fabric communication, and production file management.

Our Process

A clear workflow that keeps buyers informed at every stage.

01

Buyer Inquiry

We review product details, quantity, target price, fabric, trims, compliance needs, and delivery expectations.

02

Sample Development

We coordinate prototype, fit, size-set, photo, and approval samples with factories and suppliers.

03

Price Negotiation

We support costing analysis, factory quotation review, and buyer-factory price alignment.

04

Production Planning

We follow T&A plans, material booking, approval status, cutting, sewing, finishing, and packing progress.

05

Quality Inspection

We coordinate inspection checkpoints and report issues clearly so corrective action can be taken quickly.

06

Shipment Coordination

We support packing, booking, documents, forwarder communication, and shipment status updates.
